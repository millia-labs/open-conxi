# SOP 01: Check-in

Owner: front office manager, and the shift supervisor on duty.
Used by: guest-messages, morning-flash.
When: every arriving guest, from the moment they walk through the door to the moment they walk away with a key.

## Standard
- Greet within 10 seconds of eye contact, even if you are on the phone or serving someone else.
- Photo ID matches the name on the booking before a key is cut. No match, no key.
- Guest at the desk under 5 minutes from greeting to key in hand.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Before the shift, read the arrivals list and flag VIPs, repeats, early arrivals and special requests. VIPs go to SOP 04.
2. Look up and smile as soon as a guest catches your eye. If you are busy, a nod and "I'll be right with you" counts as the greeting.
3. Ask for the surname, pull the booking, then ask for photo ID. Check the photo against the face and the name against the booking.
4. Confirm out loud the room type, number of nights, rate and breakfast. Settle any difference now, not at check-out.
5. Take a card pre-authorisation or deposit per [your hotel: deposit policy]. Say the amount and that it is released at check-out.
6. Cut the key and hand it over in its sleeve. Point to the room number written on the sleeve, never say it aloud (SOP 07).
7. Give three facts only: breakfast time and place, wifi, how to reach the desk. Offer help with bags.
8. Room not ready: store the bags, take a mobile number, give a clock time you will message by, and keep that time.
9. Note anything you learned (anniversary, allergy, late checkout ask) in the guest profile before the next guest.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before the key leaves your hand.
- [ ] ID seen and matched to the booking name.
- [ ] Rate, nights and inclusions confirmed with the guest.
- [ ] Pre-authorisation or deposit taken and the amount stated.
- [ ] Room number on the sleeve, not spoken.
- [ ] Room shows as inspected and ready in the PMS (SOP 10).

## Escalate to the GM when
The booking is missing and the hotel is full, a guest refuses ID, the rate dispute is above your comp authority in hotel-profile.md, or the queue passes 5 guests for more than 10 minutes.

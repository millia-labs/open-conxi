# SOP 02: Check-out

Owner: front office manager, and the shift supervisor on duty.
Used by: guest-messages.
When: every departing guest, at the desk, by express check-out, or when a guest leaves without stopping.

## Standard
- Folio reviewed with the guest, line by line if they want, before payment is taken.
- Any dispute settled on the spot within comp authority from hotel-profile.md; above it, a manager decides before the guest leaves.
- Pre-authorisation released the same day and the guest told the release amount.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. The evening before, print or check tomorrow's departure folios. Look for unposted minibar, restaurant or laundry charges and post them now.
2. Greet the guest by name and ask how the stay was. Listen to the answer; a complaint here goes to SOP 05 first.
3. Ask for the keys back and check the room number against the folio.
4. Show the folio. Let the guest read it without rushing. Answer each question with the voucher or the posting time.
5. Disputed charge: if it is within your comp authority (people.comp_authority.front_desk), remove it and note why. Above it, call the manager now.
6. Take payment, then release the pre-authorisation in the same transaction or right after. Tell the guest the amount and that the bank decides how fast it shows.
7. Offer bag storage, transport and the receipt by email. Thank them by name.
8. Mark the room vacant dirty in the PMS so housekeeping sees it within minutes.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before the guest leaves the desk.
- [ ] Folio balance is zero or on the right account.
- [ ] Every adjustment has a reason code and your name on it.
- [ ] Pre-authorisation released or captured, not left hanging.
- [ ] Room status changed in the PMS.

## Escalate to the GM when
A guest refuses to pay, a dispute is above the manager's comp authority, or damage to the room is claimed or suspected.

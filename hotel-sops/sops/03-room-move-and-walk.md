# SOP 03: Room move and walking a guest

Owner: duty manager.
Used by: morning-flash, hotel-routine.
When: the hotel is oversold for tonight, or a guest must leave their room (defect, leak, noise, safety).

## Standard
- Walk decision made by 16:00 on the day of the oversell.
- Never walk a VIP, a repeat guest or a multi-night guest.
- The hotel pays night one elsewhere, transport both ways and one phone call; the guest is offered return the next day.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. At the morning brief, count tonight: rooms on the books plus expected walk-ins minus rooms available. Oversold by any number goes on the flash as a risk.
2. By 16:00, if still oversold, pick the walk: a one-night, non-VIP, non-repeat guest arriving late. Never a guest already in house.
3. Book a comparable or better room at a partner hotel before you tell anyone. Get the confirmation number and the name of the person who took it.
4. The manager tells the guest in person, face to face, at the desk. Apologise plainly. Say the hotel made the mistake.
5. Hand over the confirmation, pay the taxi there, pay the room for night one, and offer one phone call or message on the hotel.
6. Offer return the next day with an upgrade if available, and a taxi back. Note it on the booking so the desk greets them by name.
7. Room move in house: offer it before anything else, move belongings with the guest present, recut keys and void the old ones.
8. Log it: who, why, partner hotel, cost, what was offered. Cost goes against the rooms department.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before you speak to the guest.
- [ ] Guest picked is one night, not VIP, not repeat.
- [ ] Partner room confirmed with a number and a name.
- [ ] Transport arranged and paid.
- [ ] Return offer and upgrade noted on the booking.

## Escalate to the GM when
More than 2 rooms need walking in one night, no partner hotel has a comparable room, or the only guest left to walk is a VIP or repeat.

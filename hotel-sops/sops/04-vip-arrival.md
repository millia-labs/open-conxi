# SOP 04: VIP arrival

Owner: duty manager.
Used by: morning-flash, turnover-board.
When: any arrival flagged VIP by the GM, sales or the booking, and any guest with an open complaint from a past stay.

## Standard
- VIP list read out at the morning brief, with one action per VIP.
- Room inspected by a manager 2 hours before arrival, not only by housekeeping.
- Guest greeted by name at arrival; preferences from the last stay applied in the room.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. The day before, pull the VIP list from the PMS and read each guest profile: past stays, preferences, complaints, allergies, who they are with.
2. At the morning brief, name each VIP, their arrival time, their room and the one thing the team must do for them.
3. Assign the room early and block it so it cannot be moved. Tell housekeeping it is a priority clean (turnover-board flags it).
4. Apply last stay's preferences: pillow, floor, twin or king, minibar, newspaper. If there is no history, do the standard amenity per [your hotel: VIP amenity levels].
5. 2 hours before arrival, a manager walks the room: lights, aircon, bathroom, amenity, welcome card with the right name spelled right.
6. Tell the door staff and the desk the name and expected time. The first person who sees the guest uses their name.
7. Pre-register so check-in takes under 2 minutes, or check in at the room if [your hotel: in-room check-in rule] allows.
8. Within 1 hour of arrival, call the room once to ask if all is right. Log the answer and anything new you learned.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, 2 hours before arrival.
- [ ] Manager has walked the room and signed off.
- [ ] Past preferences applied, or the standard amenity placed.
- [ ] Welcome card name checked against the booking spelling.
- [ ] Desk and door know the name and arrival time.

## Escalate to the GM when
The VIP has an open complaint from a past stay, the blocked room fails inspection with under 2 hours to go, or the VIP asks for something above the manager's comp authority in hotel-profile.md.

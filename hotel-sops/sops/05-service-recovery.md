# SOP 05: Service recovery

Owner: everyone on shift; the duty manager owns the follow-up.
Used by: review-replies, guest-messages.
When: a guest complains in person, by phone, by message, or you see something go wrong before they do.

## Standard
- Listen, empathise, apologise, react, notify, in that order.
- Fixed on the spot within your comp authority from hotel-profile.md (people.comp_authority), never "I'll ask someone".
- The duty manager is told the same shift, and the fix is logged before the shift ends.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Listen. Stop what you are doing, face the guest, let them finish. Do not interrupt or explain.
2. Empathise. Say back what went wrong in their words so they know you heard it.
3. Apologise once, plainly, for what happened. Do not blame a colleague, a system or another department.
4. React. Fix the thing now if you can: move the room, send engineering, remake the dish. Say what you will do and by what clock time.
5. If a fix alone is not enough, offer something within your comp authority. Front desk and managers each have their own limit in hotel-profile.md. Above yours, get the manager now, while the guest waits.
6. Notify. Tell the duty manager before the shift ends, and log it in the guest profile: what happened, what was done, what it cost.
7. Follow up within 2 hours or before the guest leaves, whichever is sooner. Ask if the fix held.
8. If the cause is a defect, raise a work order (SOP 13) so the next guest does not get it.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before the shift ends.
- [ ] The guest was told what would happen and by when, and it happened.
- [ ] Any comp given is inside the giver's limit and logged with a reason.
- [ ] The duty manager knows.
- [ ] Follow-up done or handed over by name in the shift log (SOP 12).

## Escalate to the GM when
The complaint involves injury, theft, discrimination, a named staff member, a refund demand, or a mention of a lawyer, regulator or the press.

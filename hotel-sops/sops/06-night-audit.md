# SOP 06: Night audit

Owner: night auditor, checked by the front office manager each morning.
Used by: morning-flash.
When: every night, starting after the last expected arrival or at [your hotel: audit start time].

## Standard
- Room and tax posted to every in-house folio.
- No-shows processed under the booking's guarantee and cancellation terms.
- Rate discrepancies listed, and the manager's report run before 03:00.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Check every arrival: checked in, cancelled, or still due. Call the guarantee number for any late arrival not yet in.
2. Process no-shows: charge per the guarantee, release the room, note the booking. Do not charge a booking with no guarantee.
3. Compare every in-house room's posted rate against the booked rate. List each difference with room, booked rate, posted rate and who changed it.
4. Match room status: every occupied room in the PMS has a guest, every vacant room has none. List any room housekeeping and the desk disagree on.
5. Balance the cashier shifts: cash, cards and city ledger against the PMS. Note any over or short with the cashier's name.
6. Post room and tax, then run the day end in the PMS.
7. Run and save the manager's report, the trial balance and the high balance report before 03:00.
8. Leave a note for the morning: no-shows, rate discrepancies, high balances, and anything a guest said overnight.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before running the day end.
- [ ] Every arrival accounted for.
- [ ] No-shows charged only where guaranteed.
- [ ] Rate discrepancy list written, even if it is empty.
- [ ] Cashier shifts balanced or the difference named.

## Escalate to the GM when
The day end fails, cash is short by more than [your hotel: cash variance limit], or a guest folio is over its credit limit with no card on file.

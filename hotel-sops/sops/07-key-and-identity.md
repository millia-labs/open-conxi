# SOP 07: Keys and guest identity

Owner: front office manager.
Used by: guest-messages.
When: any key cut, replaced or handed over, any caller or visitor asking for a guest, and every master key issued.

## Standard
- A room number is never said aloud, on the phone or at the desk.
- No key without photo ID, or a match to the registered guest in the PMS.
- Master keys signed out and signed back in, by name and time, every time.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Lost key at the desk: ask for the name and photo ID, and check both against the PMS. No ID, ask two facts only the guest knows (dates, card last four digits) and check them.
2. Still no match: do not cut a key. Offer to walk the person to the room with security and have the guest open it.
3. Cut the new key and void every old key to that room in the lock system.
4. Write the room number on the sleeve and point to it. If someone at the desk can hear, never read it out.
5. Caller asks for a guest: never confirm the guest is staying. Offer to put the call through or take a message.
6. Visitor asks for a room number: do not give it. Call the guest and let them come down or say who may go up.
7. Master keys come out of [your hotel: key cabinet or safe] only against a signature, name and time. Check they come back at shift end.
8. A missing master key: tell the duty manager now and reset the locks it opens the same day.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, at every shift change.
- [ ] Every master key signed out is signed back in, or its holder is named.
- [ ] Every replacement key this shift has an ID note on the booking.
- [ ] Old keys voided for every room with a new key.

## Escalate to the GM when
A master key is missing, a guest says someone entered their room, or anyone pressures the desk for a guest's room number.

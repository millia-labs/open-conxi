# SOP 08: Departure clean

Owner: executive housekeeper, and the floor supervisor on shift.
Used by: turnover-board.
When: a guest has checked out and the room is on your board as a departure.

## Standard
- One credit is 30 minutes. A standard room departure is 1 credit; a suite or family room is 1.5.
- Same order every time: strip and air, bathroom top to bottom, bed, dust high to low, restock, floor last working back to the door.
- The room is not finished until you have checked it from the door and posted the photo set for SOP 10.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Knock three times, say "Housekeeping", wait, then enter. Door wedged open, cart across the doorway.
2. Strip and air. Open the curtains and windows, turn the aircon to [your hotel: airing setting], strip all linen and towels into the bag, empty every bin, and pull any rubbish out of drawers and the safe.
3. Walk the room for anything left behind: under the bed, the wardrobe, the safe, the bathroom hook. Anything found goes to SOP 11 before you clean.
4. Bathroom top to bottom. Spray first so the chemical has time to work; mirror, then shower walls, basin, toilet last with its own cloth. Dry every surface. Floor last, working out.
5. Bed. Fresh linen, tight corners, pillows plumped and placed to [your hotel: bed standard]. Check under the mattress protector for stains.
6. Dust high to low. Lights and picture frames, then furniture tops, desk, phone, remote and switches, skirting last. Wipe every handle.
7. Restock amenities, glasses, tea tray and minibar to par. Post anything taken from the minibar so it is charged.
8. Floor last, working back to the door. Vacuum or mop from the far wall so you never walk over a clean floor.
9. Stand in the doorway and look. Then take the four photos (bed made, bathroom, desk and minibar, floor from the door), post them, and tell your supervisor the room is ready to inspect.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before you tell the supervisor the room is ready to inspect.
- [ ] Nothing from the previous guest left anywhere, safe open and empty.
- [ ] Any fault you saw (drip, bulb, stain, broken fitting) reported as a work order under SOP 13, not left as a note.
- [ ] Minibar consumption posted.
- [ ] Four photos posted for this room.

## Escalate to the GM when
You find cash, a passport, drugs, a weapon or anything that looks like evidence; the room is damaged beyond normal wear; or the room smells of smoke in a non-smoking room.

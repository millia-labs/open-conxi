# SOP 10: Inspect and release

Owner: housekeeping supervisor on shift.
Used by: turnover-board, hotel-routine.
When: an attendant says a departure room is ready to inspect, and before any room goes back to sale.

## Standard
- No room is ready until the four-photo set is posted and a supervisor has ticked the nine-item inspection list.
- The four photos: bed made, bathroom, desk and minibar, floor from the door.
- The supervisor, never the attendant, sets the PMS status to ready.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Go to rooms in board order: same-day arrivals first, earliest arrival first, then other departures.
2. Check the four photos are posted for this room before you walk in. No photos, send the attendant back.
3. Stand in the doorway. Look at the room the way the guest will see it: smell, light, the bed, the floor.
4. Walk the nine-item list, touching as you go: bathroom clean and dry, bed linen fresh and tight, no hair on any surface, amenities at par, bins empty and lined, wifi and TV on, aircon or heating set to the standard, safe open and empty, nothing left from the previous guest.
5. Add the extra items for this room type: cot check for family rooms, kitchenette for suites, and any special request on the board (cot, allergy, connecting door).
6. Fail on any item: tell the attendant what and where, and inspect again once it is fixed. Do not fix it yourself and pass it.
7. Any fault seen goes in as a work order under SOP 13. A fault that stops the room being sold goes to SOP 14 and the room stays off sale.
8. Tick the list with your name, then set the room to ready in the PMS yourself, at [your hotel: PMS status name].
9. Same-day arrival room released: tell the front desk the room number and the time.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before you set the room to ready in the PMS.
- [ ] Four photos posted for this room.
- [ ] Nine-item inspection ticked with your name.
- [ ] Special request for this room done.
- [ ] Every defect seen is a logged work order, not a note.
- [ ] You, not the attendant, are setting the PMS status.

## Escalate to the GM when
A same-day arrival room will not pass before the guest's arrival time, a VIP room (SOP 04) fails inspection twice, or the same attendant fails the same item three days running.

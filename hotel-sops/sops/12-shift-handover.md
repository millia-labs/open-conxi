# SOP 12: Shift handover

Owner: every department head, and the person leading each shift.
Used by: hotel-routine, staff-roster.
When: at every change of shift, in every department, and whenever the person in charge hands over early.

## Standard
- The handover is written in the log, then spoken. Nothing lives only in someone's head.
- Every open item has one named owner. "The team" and "someone" are not owners.
- The handover ends with the question "Anything I'm assuming someone else has?"

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Fifteen minutes before the end of shift, open the log at [your hotel: log location] and write the handover.
2. List every open item: rooms not yet ready, work orders open (SOP 13), out of order rooms (SOP 14), guest complaints in progress (SOP 05), lost and found not yet logged (SOP 11), VIPs due (SOP 04).
3. Next to each item write one owner by name and the time it is due. If you cannot name the owner, it stays yours until you can.
4. Note anything the next shift must watch: late arrivals, a guest who needs a callback, a room on Do Not Disturb for a long time (SOP 09), short staffing against the roster.
5. Meet the incoming lead face to face. Walk the log item by item; they read it, you do not recite from memory.
6. The incoming lead asks about anything unclear and signs the log with the time.
7. Close with the question "Anything I'm assuming someone else has?" Anything that comes up is written down with an owner before you leave.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before the outgoing lead leaves the building.
- [ ] Every open item is in the written log.
- [ ] Every open item has one named owner and a due time.
- [ ] The incoming lead has signed the log.
- [ ] "Anything I'm assuming someone else has?" was asked and answered.

## Escalate to the GM when
The incoming lead does not arrive, an open item has no owner at shift end, or a P0 work order, safety issue or guest in distress is being handed over.

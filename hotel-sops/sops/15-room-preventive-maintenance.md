# SOP 15: Room preventive maintenance

Owner: chief engineer.
Used by: work-orders.
When: every guest room, once a quarter, on the PM calendar.

## Standard
- Every room gets a full PM once a quarter. No room skips a quarter.
- Same fixed checklist every time: aircon filter and drain, plumbing and drains, sealant, lights, TV and remote, safe, door closer and lock, smoke detector test, furniture.
- PM is booked into low-occupancy weeks and logged as P3 work orders.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. At the start of each quarter, split the rooms into weekly batches and place them in low-occupancy weeks on the PM calendar.
2. Each morning, check with the desk that today's PM rooms are vacant. Set them out of service, not out of order, per SOP 14.
3. Aircon: clean or change the filter, flush the drain line, check the drain pan is dry and the unit is quiet.
4. Plumbing and drains: run every tap and the shower, time the drains, check for drips, test the flush. Reseal cracked sealant around bath, shower and basin.
5. Lights, TV and remote, safe: every bulb works, TV tunes, remote has fresh batteries, safe opens with the master and a test code.
6. Door closer and lock: door closes and latches on its own from fully open; key card reads first time; deadlock and chain work.
7. Smoke detector: press the test button and hear it. Any fault is a P0 work order before the room is sold.
8. Furniture: legs, hinges, drawers, headboard, curtain track. Tighten or log.
9. Anything not fixed on the spot becomes a work order per SOP 13. Sign the room's PM sheet and hand back to housekeeping.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before the room is handed back.
- [ ] All nine checklist items ticked on the room's PM sheet.
- [ ] Smoke detector test passed and recorded.
- [ ] Every defect found has a work order number on the sheet.
- [ ] Room status returned from out of service.

## Escalate to the GM when
The quarter will end with rooms not done, a smoke detector fails, or the same defect shows up in several rooms.

# SOP 17: Daily rate review

Owner: revenue manager, or the GM where there is none.
Used by: rate-check.
When: every morning before 11:00, and the full rate-check skill once a week on the same day.

## Standard
- Read pace against the same time last year and pickup since yesterday, date by date.
- No rate cut inside 7 days of arrival without the GM's say.
- Only public rates and the hotel's own extranet data. Never competitors' non-public data.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Pull the pace report from [your hotel: PMS report] for the next 30 days at least: rooms and rate on the books.
2. Pickup: for each date, rooms on the books today minus yesterday. Mark any date that moved more than [your hotel: pickup alert] rooms either way.
3. Pace: compare rooms on the books with the same date and lead time last year. Missing last year: say so and read pickup alone.
4. Check the comp set only in the Booking.com and Expedia extranet tools or public rates. Never ask another hotel for rates or occupancy.
5. Compression date: consider a rise and close discount plans, per the rate-check skill. Normal date: hold.
6. Soft date: open inventory and cheaper channels first. A cut is the last lever, and inside 7 days it needs the GM first.
7. Group or event date: flag it for the GM. Do not auto-price it.
8. Make changes in the channel manager, then check parity on every channel. Log each change: date, old rate, new rate, reason.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before any rate goes live.
- [ ] Each change names the date, old rate, new rate and reason.
- [ ] No cut inside 7 days without the GM's name on it.
- [ ] No competitor non-public data used.
- [ ] Same rate on every channel before promotions.

## Escalate to the GM when
A cut inside 7 days looks needed, a date is 20 percent behind last year's pace, or a group or event date is in play.

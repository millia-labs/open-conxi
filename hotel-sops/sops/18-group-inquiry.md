# SOP 18: Group inquiry

Owner: sales manager, or the GM where there is none.
Used by: group-displacement.
When: any request for 8 rooms or more on the same dates: corporate, wedding, tour, sports team.

## Standard
- Reply within 24 hours of the inquiry, even if only to say when the quote will come.
- Displacement checked first, per the group-displacement skill, before any rate is quoted.
- Every contract names the cutoff date, attrition and deposit.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Log the inquiry in [your hotel: sales log]: dates, rooms per night, rate asked, F&B and meeting needs, decision date, contact.
2. Missing dates, rooms or rate: ask for all three in one reply. No quote until you have them.
3. Acknowledge within 24 hours with the date the quote will reach them.
4. Run the group-displacement skill: nightly transient forecast, displaced rooms, opportunity cost, group contribution, floor rate.
5. Contribution beats opportunity cost by 10 percent or more: quote. Within 10 percent: counter at the floor rate. Below: decline or offer other dates.
6. Quote with terms: cutoff date (default 30 days before arrival), attrition (default 10 percent), deposit (default 25 percent at contract). Mark defaults as defaults until agreed.
7. Contract signed and deposit received: block the rooms in the PMS with the cutoff date on the block.
8. At cutoff, release unpicked rooms to general sale the same day.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, before a quote leaves the hotel.
- [ ] Displacement run for every night, summed, not averaged.
- [ ] Floor rate stated as a number.
- [ ] Cutoff date, attrition and deposit written in.
- [ ] Decision date falls before the cutoff the hotel needs.

## Escalate to the GM when
The quote is below the floor rate, the group takes more than [your hotel: group ceiling] percent of keys on any night, or the contract changes the default terms.

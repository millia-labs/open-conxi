# SOP 19: Fire alarm

Owner: duty manager.
Used by: hotel-routine.
When: the fire alarm sounds, a fire or smoke is reported, or the panel shows a fire signal.

## Standard
- The hotel's fire plan and local law come first. This page supports them.
- In-house list and the list of guests needing help printed at once.
- Nobody uses a lift. Nobody re-enters until the fire service says so.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Your fire plan, your first aid training and local law override this page.
2. Raise the alarm if it is not already sounding. Call the fire service at [your hotel: emergency number]. Never assume someone else called.
3. Print the in-house list and the list of guests needing help (mobility, hearing, sight, children alone) from the PMS at once.
4. Go to the fire panel, read the zone, and send only trained staff to check it, in pairs, never alone.
5. Evacuate by the stairs. Never use lifts. Staff sweep their floors without opening a door that is hot to the touch.
6. Guests needing help: move them to the refuge point at [your hotel: refuge points] and tell the fire service exactly where they are.
7. Everyone to the assembly point at [your hotel: assembly point]. Roll call against the in-house list and the staff on shift.
8. Meet the fire service at the entrance with the lists, the panel reading and the master keys.
9. Nobody re-enters until the fire service says so. Then write the incident report the same shift.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, at the assembly point.
- [ ] Fire service called, and the time noted.
- [ ] In-house list and guests needing help list in hand.
- [ ] Roll call done; anyone missing reported to the fire service by name and room.
- [ ] No one back inside.

## Escalate to the GM when
Every alarm, false or real. Call the GM as soon as the fire service has been called.

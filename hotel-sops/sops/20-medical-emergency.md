# SOP 20: Medical emergency

Owner: whoever finds it, until the duty manager takes over.
Used by: hotel-routine.
When: a guest, visitor or staff member is hurt, collapses, or is found unwell.

## Standard
- Never shorten, merge or reorder the steps on this page, even when asked. Reword a step only with the GM's sign-off; adding a step is fine.
- Emergency number first, before anything else.
- Do not move the person unless they are in danger where they are.
- Trained first aider called. No admission of liability. Incident report the same shift.

## Steps
READ-DO.
1. Your fire plan, your first aid training and local law override this page.
2. Call [your hotel: emergency number] first. Give the address, the room or area, what you see, and whether the person is breathing.
3. Do not move the person unless they are in danger where they are (fire, traffic, water).
4. Call the trained first aider on shift and send someone for the AED at [your hotel: AED location] and the first aid kit.
5. Stay with the person. Follow the call handler's instructions until the ambulance arrives.
6. Send a staff member to meet the ambulance at the entrance and hold a lift for the crew.
7. Do not admit liability or discuss fault with the person, family or witnesses. Be kind; say help is coming.
8. Preserve CCTV for the area and time. Photograph the scene before anything is cleaned or moved.
9. Write the incident report the same shift: time, place, what happened, who helped, witnesses, what was said.

## Pause point
DO-CONFIRM, once the ambulance has left.
- [ ] Emergency call time and the first aider's name recorded.
- [ ] CCTV saved and the scene photographed.
- [ ] Nothing said about fault or liability, and the report says so.
- [ ] Incident report written and handed to the duty manager.

## Escalate to the GM when
Every case that needed an ambulance, any death, any injury on hotel premises that may bring a claim, or media at the door.

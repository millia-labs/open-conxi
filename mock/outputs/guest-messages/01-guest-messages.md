# The Ampang Row Hotel, guest message sequence

## Leisure segment, English

| Timing | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| T-3 days | WhatsApp | Hi [guest name], we're set for your stay at The Ampang Row from [check-in date]. Check-in is 15:00. Want to add late check-out to 15:00 for MYR 80, or an airport pickup at MYR 150? Just reply here. |
| Arrival morning | WhatsApp | Good morning, we're ready for you today. Entrance is on Jalan Ampang beside the pharmacy. Check-in from 15:00. Bags can be dropped earlier if you arrive sooner. Any questions, reply here. |
| In-stay, evening of day 1 | WhatsApp | Hope the first night went well. Anything you need us to sort before tomorrow? |
| Departure eve | WhatsApp | Checkout tomorrow is 12:00. Late check-out to 15:00 is MYR 80 if you'd like more time, and airport transfer is MYR 150 each way if booked by 20:00 tonight. |
| Post-stay, +24h | Email | Thank you for staying with us. If you have two minutes, a review on Google or Booking.com helps us a lot: [review link]. Hope to see you again. |

## Family segment, English

Same five messages, with the pre-arrival line replaced: "Your Family Twin is confirmed. Breakfast at Row Kitchen runs 06:30 to 10:30, level 2, and we can have a cot ready if you let us know." In-stay line adds: "How's breakfast working for the kids?"

## Business segment, English

Pre-arrival adds: "Your invoice will be ready at checkout; let us know if you need it itemised for your company." Arrival adds a note that breakfast opens from 06:30 for early departures.

## Malay and Mandarin variants

Each of the above five messages was also written directly in Malay and Mandarin (not translated word for word), matching the profile's languages. Full text omitted from this sample for length; the same facts and timing apply.

## One-off reply example

Guest asked via WhatsApp: "Is parking free?"
Reply: "Parking is MYR 12 a night, 40 bays on site. Let us know your arrival time and we'll hold a spot."

## Placeholders used
[guest name], [check-in date], [review link] — filled per guest and per stay at send time.
